# Pricing Page Conversion Audit

## Inputs to gather
- Current pricing page content/screenshot or pricing tiers as text
- Target customer segments per tier
- Known conversion problems (e.g., "everyone picks the cheapest tier", "people ask what's the difference")

## Audit checklist
1. **Tier count & naming**: 3–4 tiers is usually the sweet spot; check names communicate who each tier is for.
2. **Anchoring**: is there a clear "most popular" or highlighted tier steering the decision?
3. **Feature list clarity**: are differences between tiers scannable, or does the user have to read every line to find the difference?
4. **Price framing**: monthly vs annual toggle, per-seat vs flat, and whether the framing matches how the customer thinks about value.
5. **FAQ section**: does it pre-empt real objections (billing, cancellation, overage) or is it generic filler?
6. **CTA clarity**: is the primary action per tier unambiguous (Start free trial / Talk to sales / Buy now)?
7. **Currency/localization**: if serving multiple regions, check whether pricing is localized or just currency-converted awkwardly.

## Output
- A findings list ranked by expected conversion impact (High/Medium/Low), each with a concrete before → after fix.
- Do not invent conversion data or benchmarks you weren't given — describe directional reasoning instead ("removing this ambiguity typically reduces support questions and hesitation").
