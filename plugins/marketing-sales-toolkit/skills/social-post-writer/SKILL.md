# Social Post Writer

Write one or more short social posts tailored to the platform requested.

## Inputs to gather
- Product/offer/announcement being promoted
- Target platform(s) — tone and length differ per platform
- Target audience (role, industry, pain point)
- Call to action (link, sign-up, reply, DM)

## Platform rules
- **LinkedIn**: 3–5 short paragraphs, professional but human, no more than 1–2 emojis, end with a question or CTA that invites comments.
- **X (Twitter)**: under 280 characters, punchy hook in the first line, at most 1 hashtag.
- **Instagram**: hook line + short story + CTA + up to 8 relevant hashtags in a separate block.
- **Facebook**: conversational, 2–4 sentences, works well with a direct question.

## Process
1. Identify the single strongest benefit or hook — do not list every feature.
2. Draft 3 variants with different hooks (curiosity, pain point, social proof).
3. Keep claims accurate to what the user told you — never invent metrics, testimonials, or numbers.
4. Output each variant labeled by platform and hook angle, ready to copy-paste.
