# Email Subject Line Optimizer

## Inputs to gather
- Email content/offer summary
- Audience segment (cold prospect, existing customer, newsletter subscriber)
- Any brand tone constraints (formal, playful, plain)

## Process
1. Draft 8–10 subject line candidates spanning these angles: curiosity, direct benefit, urgency/scarcity, personalization, question, numbers/specificity.
2. Keep each under 60 characters (most clients truncate around 40–60).
3. Avoid spam-trigger words (FREE!!!, ALL CAPS, excessive punctuation) and avoid clickbait that the email body doesn't deliver on.
4. Pair each subject line with matching preview text (max ~90 characters) that adds new information rather than repeating the subject.
5. Mark your top 3 picks and explain in one line each why they should perform best for the stated audience.
