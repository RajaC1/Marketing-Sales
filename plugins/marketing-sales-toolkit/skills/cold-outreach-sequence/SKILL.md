# Cold Outreach Sequence Writer

## Inputs to gather
- Ideal customer profile (role, industry, company size)
- The specific pain point or trigger event being targeted
- Offer/product and its core value proposition
- Number of touches wanted (default: 4 emails + 1 LinkedIn touch over 2–3 weeks)

## Sequence structure (default 4-touch)
1. **Touch 1 — Pattern interrupt**: short, personalized, one clear pain point, one soft CTA (question, not a meeting ask).
2. **Touch 2 — Value add** (3–4 days later): share a relevant insight, stat, or resource; no hard pitch.
3. **Touch 3 — Social proof** (4–5 days later): brief, relevant customer result; direct CTA for a call.
4. **Touch 4 — Breakup email** (5–7 days later): short, low-pressure, gives them an easy out, often gets the highest reply rate.

## Rules
- Every email under 120 words. Sales prospects skim.
- One CTA per email — never stack multiple asks.
- Personalization tokens should be marked clearly (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `{{trigger_event}}`) so the user can merge-fill them.
- Do not fabricate case studies, logos, or stats — use placeholders like `[insert customer result]` if none were provided.
- Flag legal/compliance considerations (e.g., CAN-SPAM, GDPR unsubscribe language) if the user is sending at scale.
